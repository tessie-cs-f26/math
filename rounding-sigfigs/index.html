Rounding Sig Figs

Two measurements, one operation. Give the answer rounded the way the data allows.

The rules
  • Adding or subtracting: round to the last decimal place of the least precise measurement.
  • Multiplying or dividing: keep as many significant figures as the measurement with the fewest.
  • Trailing zeros count. If the answer should be 6.0, typing 6 is marked wrong.
