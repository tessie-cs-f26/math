xm/n

Exponent Sprint

Evaluate and simplify powers with fractional exponents. Pick the right answer from four choices.

The rules
  • The denominator is the root and the numerator is the power: xm/n means take the n-th root of x, then raise it to the m-th power.
  • A negative exponent means reciprocal: x−m/n = 1 ÷ xm/n.
  • An odd root of a negative number is negative: the cube root of −8 is −2.
  • With the same base, multiply by adding exponents, divide by subtracting them, and raise a power to a power by multiplying them.
  • Variables stand for positive numbers. Answers are written with positive exponents only.
